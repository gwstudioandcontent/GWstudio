Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

1. Summary

GW Clip Studio runs only on the owner's own computer. It has no user accounts, no public sign-up and no remote server that stores user data. We do not sell or share data with anyone.

2. Data we receive from TikTok

  • Basic profile (user.info.basic): open ID, display name and avatar of the connected account, used only to show which account is connected.
  • Access tokens: issued by TikTok after the owner logs in, used only to upload and publish the owner's approved videos (video.upload, video.publish).

The app never sees or stores TikTok passwords. Login happens on TikTok's official page.

3. Data from other platforms

For YouTube and Facebook Pages the app receives the channel or Page name, picture, follower count and access tokens, used for the same purpose: publishing the owner's videos and showing basic statistics to the owner.

4. Where data is stored

All tokens and profile information are stored locally on the owner's computer. They are not uploaded to any server other than the platform the data came from.

5. Sharing

Data is not sold, rented or shared with third parties. It is sent only to the platform it belongs to (for example, a video is sent to TikTok when it is published to TikTok).

6. Retention and deletion

Data is kept until the owner disconnects the account. Disconnecting in the app deletes the stored token and profile information from the computer. Access can also be revoked at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children and is not used to collect data from children.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through the contact options on our YouTube channel.
