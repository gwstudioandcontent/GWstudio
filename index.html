GW Clip Studio

GW Clip Studio is a personal desktop application that runs on the owner's own computer. It researches trending topics, creates short vertical videos (10, 30 and 60 seconds) with Thai narration and subtitles, and lets the owner review each video before it is published.

How it works

  1. The app finds trending topics and writes a short script.
  2. It renders a vertical video with voice-over, subtitles and stock footage.
  3. The owner reviews the video and approves it in the dashboard.
  4. Approved videos are published to the owner's own connected accounts (TikTok, YouTube, Facebook Pages) at a scheduled time.

TikTok integration

  • Login Kit – the owner connects their own TikTok account by logging in on TikTok's official page. The app never sees the password.
  • user.info.basic – shows the connected account's display name and avatar in the dashboard so the owner knows which account a video will go to.
  • video.upload / video.publish – uploads and publishes only the videos the owner has approved, with the caption and privacy setting the owner chose.

The owner can disconnect an account at any time from the dashboard or from TikTok settings.
